Voxel Rush - Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Purchases and Restore

1. I bought an upgrade but my progress reset. Why?

Your game progress (coins, unlocked skins, scores and settings) is saved locally on your device. Reinstalling the game, clearing its data, or switching to a new phone resets that progress.

However, your purchased packs are safe. They are tied to your Google Play account, and you can get them back by following the steps in question 2.

2. How do I restore my purchases on a new phone?
  1. Make sure your Android phone is signed in to the same Google Play account you used to buy the pack.
  2. Open Voxel Rush and go to the Store screen.
  3. Tap the Restore Purchases button.
  4. The game checks your Google Play account and re-activates what you own, and shows a message when it is done.

The game also checks this quietly each time it starts, so your purchases often come back automatically.

3. What exactly gets restored?

Restore re-activates your one-time purchases:

  • Coin Vault - ad-free benefits
  • Mega Bundle and Mega Bundle Upgrade - the permanent 2x coin multiplier, the exclusive VIP skins and ad-free benefits

Coins are not restored, because they are part of your local game progress (see question 1). Coin Crate purchases are repeatable consumables, so they cannot be restored either. If you lose progress on a device, the coins from earlier purchases are lost with it.

4. I have several Google accounts on my phone. Which one holds my purchase?

Purchases belong to the Google Play account that made the purchase. Make sure that account is added on your phone (Settings > Accounts > Google) and is the active account in the Google Play Store app, then tap Restore Purchases again.

5. The "Restore Purchases" button did not work. What should I do?
  1. Check that you have an internet connection.
  2. Go to your phone's Settings > Accounts > Google and verify that the email address where you received your original Google Play receipt is signed in.
  3. Open the Google Play Store app once, so your account is active.
  4. Close Voxel Rush completely, reopen it, go to the Store and tap Restore Purchases again.

If it still does not work, contact us (see the bottom of this page) and include your Google Play order number.

6. Will I be charged twice if I tap "Buy" again on a new phone?

For one-time packs (Coin Vault, Mega Bundle, Mega Bundle Upgrade): no. If you are signed in to the same Google Play account, Google Play will tell you that you already own the item and will not charge you again. In that case, use Restore Purchases instead.

The Coin Crate is different. It is designed to be bought as many times as you like, and each purchase is charged and gives you 10,000 coins.

7. Why do I no longer see the Coin Vault in the Store?

The Store only shows packs that make sense for you. If you own the Mega Bundle, the Coin Vault is hidden because the Mega Bundle already includes everything in it. If you own the Coin Vault, you will see the cheaper Mega Bundle Upgrade instead.

Ads

8. How do I remove ads?

Buying the Coin Vault or the Mega Bundle removes banner ads from the game.

9. The rewarded ad did not give me coins. What should I do?

Watch the ad until the end and wait for it to close completely. If you close it early, no reward is given. If there is no ad available, try again after a short while, as ads depend on your internet connection and availability.
